GPT-3 Learns New Tricks On The Fly

6 August 2020 threadoriginal

These experiments by @MelMitchell1 are quite surreal. Given that GPT-3 training data didn’t had any of these examples (which is why 1-5 shot learning), this online generalization is truly bewildering!

https://medium.com/@melaniemitchell.me/can-gpt-3-make-analogies-16436605c446
